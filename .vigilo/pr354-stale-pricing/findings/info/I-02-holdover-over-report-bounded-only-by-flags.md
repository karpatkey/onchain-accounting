# I-02 — Up to 7 days of stale holdover can over-report, and for monitor-less assets only `stale` signals it
- Design acceptance (ADR 0029), recorded for the reviewer. A primary that freezes during a fast market move (or a stale debt leg in the other direction) keeps its last price for up to MAX_STALE_PRICE_AGE. `irregular` catches drift only for assets with fresh monitors; many primaries have none (e.g. mainnet eETH, WBTC, COMP, Base cbBTC). TwoFeedChainlinkPriceFeed rate legs can likewise be up to 7 days old (previously capped at RATE_HEARTBEAT, typically 86400). The stale price is not manipulable (it is the last on-chain oracle answer; the ranking is freshest-timestamp within a tier and cannot be steered by an attacker). Consumers must gate on `stalePriceAssets` / `quoteAssetStale` / `healthyFeedCount`. Not a defect.
