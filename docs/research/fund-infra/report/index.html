FundConfig: one declaration, derived permissions and accounting

A fund declares its positions once. Each declared (module, instance) yields both the Zodiac Roles v2 leaves the manager may call and the balance-adapter route that values it. Mainnet fork at block 26,500,000.

How the derivation works

Outside FundConfig: the Safe's owners can still enable another module, set a guard, or remove Roles.

Shared functions merged

  • approve on USDC: 5 positions → one scopeFunction with an OR of their conditions
  • approve on WETH: 3 positions → one scopeFunction with an OR of their conditions
  • exactInputSingle on Uniswap SwapRouter02: 2 positions → one scopeFunction with an OR of their conditions

Known gaps per type

  • aave-v3-borrow: no LTV / health-factor bound in Roles; the removal guard is per asset, so a co-declared supply of the same asset blocks removing the borrow.
  • uniswap-v3-swap: single-hop only; slippage bound is only the caller's amountOutMinimum.
  • curve pool + gauge: two declared positions because accounting is one route per position; legacy Curve pools report no wallet LP through CurvePoolsMeta (latent, in src/).

Can the NAV be read on subscription?

Cold reads (first touch in the tx), mainnet fork. Red bars do not fit in one transaction.

1. Sync: NAV inside each subscriptionfeasiblePer-tx cost is the cold read; warm figures apply only within one tx after the first read. Subscription tx needs headroom above NAV for the mint/deposit path and the 16,777,216 cap. Production accounts 2, 9, 10 already exceed the cap cold at 26.5M.
2. Async batch: NAV once per settlementfeasibleBatching cuts per-request gas, not the per-tx ceiling; the settlement tx must still fit. Needed when user-paid sync NAV gas per subscription is too high, not when the cap binds.
3. Oracle: pushed NAV snapshotfeasible · not optimalKeeper/oracle update txs are paid on a cadence, on top of NAV computation (off-chain or on-chain to post). Price is as old as the last push, which opens NAV-timing arbitrage; bound it with staleness checks. Fallback when the cap binds: a pushed snapshot avoids the single-tx limit for large accounts.

fundNav cost per position depends heavily on type (funded Aave ≈ 1,143,239 vs Morpho ≈ 120,807 marginal); the POC's fundNav prices each leg separately and is not optimized, so these are upper bounds for the scoped design.

Standard proposal: NAV-linked tokenized funds

Three interfaces on the fund's share token, against existing ERCs (detail: architecture-comparison.md, erc-landscape.md). Green = where no ERC exists and kpk already has the substance.

(a) Fund discoveryshare token → metadata, NAV sourcePartly covered: ERC-7575, ERC-8318 draft. Missing: share-token binding.
(b) NAV-bound settlementrequests settle only against an authorized NAV, min-outGap: ERC-7540 has no min-out by design; ERC-8330 snapshots exist. Keep as kpk interface.
(c) Portfolio accountingper-position assets, debt, protocol exposureUncovered by any ERC. kpk's PositionBalance + FundConfig supply it.
